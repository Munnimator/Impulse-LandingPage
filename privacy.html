Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

ImpulseLog ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Personal Information:

  • Apple ID information (when you sign in with Apple)
  • Email address (if provided)
  • Profile information you choose to add

Financial Data:

  • Impulse purchase amounts you log
  • Savings goals and progress
  • Spending categories and descriptions
  • Mood data (optional)
  • Notes and reflection details you choose to add

Optional Place Context:

  • Place names or store names you choose to attach to a save, purchase, or shopping list
  • Coarse location information used to suggest nearby places when you explicitly request it
  • Shopping list place context and recent shopping-list history stored for app functionality

Usage Data:

  • App usage statistics
  • Feature interactions
  • Error logs and crash reports

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Generate GPT-powered insights (Premium users)
  • Track your progress and achievements
  • Help identify mood, category, and place patterns in your own logs
  • Improve app performance and features
  • Provide customer support

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • HTTPS encryption for secure data transmission
  • Firebase-managed storage and authentication infrastructure
  • Operational safeguards designed to limit unnecessary access
  • We do not sell your personal information

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Export your data
  • Delete your account and all data
  • Choose whether to add optional place context
  • Choose whether to use optional Premium GPT features

Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services. You can delete your account at any time from the app settings.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Database and authentication
  • OpenAI: GPT insights (Premium feature)
  • Apple: App Store, Apple Sign-In, and Apple Maps/MapKit place lookup features

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the "last updated" date.
